Our Ministries

Serving God & Community

At West Ruwenzori Diocese, our ministries are the heartbeat of our mission. Through worship, discipleship, evangelism, pastoral care, and community engagement, we seek to nurture spiritually mature believers.

I

Mission & Evangelism

Proclaiming the Gospel through church planting, crusades, evangelism, conferences, and outreach programmes.

II

Home Cell Ministry

Small neighborhood groups for prayer, Bible study, discipleship, and community development.

III

Children's Ministry

Nurturing children in Christian values through Sunday School, Bible teaching, and mentorship.

IV

Youth Ministry

Spiritual mentorship, leadership development, vocational guidance, and life-skills training.

V

Mothers' Union

Empowering women to strengthen Christian families through prayer, service, and outreach.

VI

Choir & Worship

Enriching worship through music, praise, and liturgy that glorifies God.
